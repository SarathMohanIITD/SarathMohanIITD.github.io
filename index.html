Building AI that ships

An ML Researcher driven by a passion for applying Generative AI, Diffusion Models, and Graph Neural Networks to high-impact domains.

At Monotype, I design and deploy next-generation generative systems that bring intelligence and automation to typographic design. My work includes AI-powered font generation — reducing production cycles from months to weeks — and building multi-modal pipelines that integrate font, text, image, and user input to deliver creative, real-world visual design tools.
